# TD 1 — Verbes, en bref

**Méthode** : mot-clé de temps, puis actif ou passif, puis conjugaison.

| Mots-clés | Temps |
|---|---|
| every year | présent simple |
| right now, at the moment | présent continu (be + -ing) |
| last year, ... ago | prétérit |
| since, for ... now, over the past few years | present perfect |
| by + date passée | past perfect |
| must, should, can | modal + base |

**Passif** = be (au bon temps) + participe passé : is exported, are being collected, were built, has been created, should be created.

| Piège | Bon réflexe |
|---|---|
| Oublier « being » au présent continu passif | are **being** collected |
| Mettre « to » après un modal | must dispose of |
| Accorder mal « awareness » | singulier : has increased |
| Oublier que le participe est irrégulier | build, built, built ; throw, threw, thrown |
